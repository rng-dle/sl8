Appearance

Theme

Default pattern

For canvases without their own look. Each canvas can have its own theme, colours, pattern and image from its ⋯ menu.

Tools

Ring is one round button you can drag anywhere; tap it and your tools open in a circle around it. Bar keeps them along the bottom. Very small screens always use the bar.

Logo colours

For the browser tab, the app icon when you install ThinkSL8, and the opening. An app you installed already picks up new colours when your browser next refreshes it, usually within a day; reinstalling it updates it now.

Hints

The short tips on an empty canvas. Turn them off and they stay off. The tour is always there under ⋯ > Show me around.

Minimap

A small map of the whole canvas in the corner. Tap or drag on it to jump there. On a canvas, press M or use the map button to switch it on and off.

Opening animation

Stretch waits for you: put a finger in each loop of the 8 (or drag one with a mouse) and pull until the page tears. Quick plays by itself. It's always off if your device asks for reduced motion.

Tear sound

No sound chosen yet

Plays as the page tears open. Your own can be any audio file up to 10 MB, like an MP3. Only its first 6 seconds play.
