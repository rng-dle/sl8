Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

ThinkSL8 is a place to keep your thoughts, so we keep as little else as we can. This page explains what we store, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and your password, stored only as a salted hash we can't reverse or read.
  • What you create: your canvases, their titles, typed text, handwriting and drawings (stored as pen strokes), where everything sits on the canvas, and when it was last changed.
  • Your AI connection, if you add one: which provider and model you chose, what you allowed the AI to read, and the key or sign-in token for that provider, encrypted. We also count how many AI requests you make each day.
  • Sharing, if you use it: the links you make, what each one allows, and which part of the canvas it covers. Anyone with a link can see or edit what it allows without an account; turning a link off ends it.
  • Security records: sign-in sessions, password-reset and email-confirmation links (all stored as hashes), whether you've confirmed your email, failed sign-in counts, and short-lived counters tied to your network address or email that stop people from guessing passwords or flooding sign-ups.
  • On your device: one cookie that keeps you signed in, and preferences such as theme, background and your last tool, plus edits that haven't reached our servers yet, in your browser's storage.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which, like any website, receives your IP address when your browser fetches them.

How we use it

Only to run ThinkSL8: to sign you in, save and show your canvases, answer the AI questions you ask, and keep accounts safe. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.

When you use AI

Nothing is sent to an AI provider until you ask something. When you do, we send your question and the typed text you allowed (your selection, the current canvas, or all canvases, as set in Settings) to the provider you connected: OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives, and any charges are between you and them. Handwriting and drawings are not sent.

Email

We email you to confirm your address when you sign up, and when you ask to reset your password. Those emails are delivered through our email provider.

Sign-up, sign-in and password reset may show a bot check from Cloudflare Turnstile, which receives your IP address and browser details to tell people from automated sign-ups.

Where it's kept

ThinkSL8 runs on Cloudflare. Your data is stored in a Cloudflare database whose primary location is in the Asia-Pacific region, and everything travels over encrypted connections.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data until you delete it. Deleting a canvas removes it straight away. Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes your account, every canvas, and your AI connection straight away. Our hosting provider keeps database backups for up to 30 days, so deleted data is fully gone after that.

Your choices

  • Download everything you've written from Settings → Account → Download my data.
  • Disconnect your AI at any time in Settings → AI.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Account.
  • For anything else, including questions about this policy, email hello@thinksl8.com.

Children

ThinkSL8 isn't meant for children under 13, and they shouldn't create an account.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
